QubitFast encrypts your text with a shared secret key, turns every 4-bit group into a qubit state on the Bloch sphere, and locks the message after 3 wrong key attempts. The sender is alerted instantly.

How it works

  1. Split The message becomes UTF-8 bytes, then 4-bit groups.
  2. Encrypt Each group is XORed with the next digit of the shared key.
  3. Map Each encrypted group picks one of 16 qubit states (angles θ and φ).
  4. Guard The receiver gets 3 tries. After that the message locks and the sender is alerted.

Try it: send a message here, open this page in a second tab, go to Inbox and enter a wrong key three times. The alert appears in the first tab.

BB84 key exchange

Alice sends random bits as qubits in random bases (+ or ×). Bob measures in random bases. They keep the rounds where the bases matched and compare half of those in public. If Eve is listening, her measurements disturb the qubits and the error rate jumps to about 25%.
